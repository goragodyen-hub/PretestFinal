เฉลย: Goals Then and Now (กล่าวถึงเป้าหมายแรกเริ่มในปี 1995 และเป้าหมาย 3 ข้อในปัจจุบัน)
เฉลย: The Growth of Google (กล่าวถึงการเติบโตอย่างรวดเร็วและการขยายบริการต่างๆ)
เฉลย: Ideas and Creativity (กล่าวถึงสถานที่ทำงานที่สร้างสรรค์และการประกวดผลงานศิลปะ)

[Paragraph 1] Google is a huge technology company. (4) It specializes in online advertising and searching, as well as other Internet-related products. Google was started by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. (5) They met at Stanford University in 1995. Their goal was to organize all of the information on the Web. Today, their company employs more than 40,000 people around the world. (6) The two partners created a company that made searching the Internet easy. Now they focus on three main areas: making search fast and smart, developing products for different devices, and helping new businesses find customers.

[Paragraph 2] Google grew very quickly. Page and Brin registered the domain name Google.com in 1997. In 1998, they set up a small office in a garage and hired their first employee, Craig Silverstein. They ran their business in the garage until they could move to a larger space. In 2000, people could do Internet searches in 15 languages. Google introduced a map service in 2005 called Google Maps. The same year, (7) it came out with a program called Google Earth. (8) This program allowed users to see close-up pictures of cities and neighborhoods when (9) they typed in an address. In 2006, the name "Google" became a verb in English dictionaries. (10) This shows the company's influence on modern life.

[Paragraph 3] Today, Google is a creative workplace where employees share ideas with (11) each other. In 2011, the company released a program that helped people explore the world's top museums from their computer. Google was also continuing its work on a self-driving car. In the future, this car could help people who cannot see well to drive. The company extends its services to the community, too. In 2008, (12) it started a yearly art contest for students. Google believes that creativity is important. In addition to producing famous Internet products, Google gives people opportunities to be creative, (13) which leads to success.

Section A: Connectors and Commas Multiple Choice (10 Marks)

Directions: Circle the letter of the option that best completes each sentence.

21. He went to the grocery store, ________.

เฉลย B: เชื่อมประโยคขัดแย้งใช้ , but โดยห้ามมี comma หลัง but

22. I washed the dishes, ________.

เฉลย A: โครงสร้าง S + V, and S + V มีความหมายคล้อยตามกันอย่างถูกต้อง

23. The bus was delayed, ________.

เฉลย C: ความสัมพันธ์เชิงเหตุ-ผล S + V, so S + V ต้องมีประธาน she ครบถ้วน

24. Thatakamol practises the piano every day, ________.

เฉลย C: ซ้อมเปียโนทุกวัน "ดังนั้น" จึงเล่นเก่งมาก ใช้ so she is really good at it

25. He went to the ________.

เฉลย A: ของ 3 สิ่งขึ้นไปตามกฎ Oxford Comma: A, B, and C

26. The puppy that belongs to that old lady is ________.

เฉลย C: คุณศัพท์ 3 ตัวแจกแจงรายการ: soft, playful, and adorable

27. Sirin has travelled to ________.

เฉลย A: ของแค่ 2 สิ่ง (A and B) ห้ามใส่ Comma เด็ดขาด

28. Pandh's favourite school subjects are ________.

เฉลย B: ของแค่ 2 สิ่ง (two things) ห้ามใส่ Comma

29. Natalie was not ready for the English test, ________.

เฉลย B: ความหมายขัดแย้งกันอย่างถูกต้อง: "ยังไม่พร้อมสอบ แต่เธออ่านหนังสือมาทั้งเช้า"

30. He studied hard, ________.

เฉลย A: "เขาตั้งใจเรียน ดังนั้นเขาจึงสอบผ่าน" (เหตุ → ผล ใช้ so he passed the exam)
Section B: Join Sentences (5 Marks)

Directions: Join two sentences with the conjunction in brackets. พิมพ์ประโยคที่ถูกต้องลงในช่องคำตอบ

31. The car is old. It runs really fast. (but)
เฉลย: The car is old, but it runs really fast. (ใส่ comma หน้า but)
32. My mom punished me. I had broken her rules. (so)
เฉลย: I had broken my mom's rules, so she punished me. (ต้องสลับเอา "เหตุ" ขึ้นก่อน "ผล")
33. We went to San Juan. All of us danced all night. (and)
เฉลย: We went to San Juan, and all of us danced all night. (ใส่ comma หน้า and)
34. Jennie felt cold during the walk. She forgot her jacket. (so)
เฉลย: Jennie forgot her jacket, so she felt cold during the walk. (สลับเอาการลืมเสื้อขึ้นก่อน)
35. Jessie does not like bananas. She loves pears. (but)
เฉลย: Jessie does not like bananas, but she loves pears.
