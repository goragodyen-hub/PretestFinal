เฉลยชุดที่ 3: แบบฝึก Verb 10 ข้อ

1. eat ➔ ate (I ate noodles yesterday.)
2. buy ➔ bought (I bought a new pen yesterday.)
3. watch ➔ watched (I watched a movie yesterday.)
4. go ➔ went (I went to school yesterday.)
5. clean ➔ cleaned (I cleaned my room yesterday.)
6. see ➔ saw (I saw a cat yesterday.)
7. read ➔ read (I read a comic book yesterday.)
8. listen ➔ listened (I listened to music yesterday.)
9. write ➔ wrote (I wrote a letter yesterday.)
10. drink ➔ drank (I drank milk yesterday.)
